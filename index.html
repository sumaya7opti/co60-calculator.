How this is calculated

SSD technique: Time (min) = (Dose × 100) ÷ (Dose rate × Output factor × %PDD). Dose is prescribed at a given depth below the skin surface, with the source fixed at a set skin distance.

SAD technique: Time (min) = Dose ÷ (Dose rate × Output factor × TMR). Dose is prescribed at the isocentre (a fixed depth from the source, independent of skin distance), so the calculation uses tissue-maximum ratio (a plain 0–1 ratio, not a percentage) instead of percentage depth dose.

In both cases, a rectangular field is first converted to its equivalent square (bilinear-interpolated from the department's rectangular-field table), and the output factor and PDD/TMR are read from the relevant tissue table at the given depth. Dose rate is decay-corrected to the treatment date.

Values outside the tables: if a field size or depth falls outside the department tables, the calculator stops and says which value is missing instead of guessing. Tick “Enter values manually” on that field to supply the equivalent square, output factor and/or %PDD (TMR). Anything left blank is still read from the tables, and manual values are marked in the results and in saved records.
